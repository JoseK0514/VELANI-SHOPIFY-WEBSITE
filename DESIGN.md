# Approved visual direction
Source: docs/reference/approved-homepage.png (second displayed mockup). User selected the recommendation with “Let's do it.”
Navy #152637, paper #F5F9FD, white #F8FBFF, pale blue #BBD8FF, muted #ADC0D6, ink #112235. DM Sans, locally hosted, weights 400–600. Official Phosphor regular icons only.
The home page has a spacious navy split hero (headline left, four service links right), a paper NEVORA product band, and a navy project invitation and full footer. Preserve the same world across every route. Do not introduce a new visual exploration.
H1 “Your business. In working software.” Primary “Discuss a project”; secondary “Explore our products.” NEVORA is In development. Illustrations are workflow concepts, not screenshots.
Desktop target is the reference's normalized 969×1622 composition; also adapt 1440/1920 and 320/390/768 viewports with natural reading and visible controls. No forced viewport heights or horizontal clipping.
Use real links and semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, readable policy typography and reduced motion. No gradients, custom CSS art, fabricated testimonials, client counts, launch claims or prices.
