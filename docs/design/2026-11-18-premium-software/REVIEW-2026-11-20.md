November 20, 2026. Skills used: design critique, better-ui (UI polish), accessibility review (WCAG), Shopify Liquid, 21st.dev inspiration. Mockups only; no theme, store or setting was changed.

## Result

## Findings and fixes

### Usability and consistency (design critique)
1. **Critical: no phone menu.** Added a full-screen menu (pattern checked against 21st.dev "Immersive Full Screen Navigation"): large type links, trial button, sign in, language, focus trap, Escape, page scroll lock, `overscroll-behavior: contain`, staggered 300ms entrance gated behind reduced motion.
2. **Header differed on every page and from the plan.** One shared `header.html`: `VELANI · NEVORA · Rondo · Relay · Plans · Support · EN | ES · Sign in · Free trial`, with the current page marked (`aria-current`).
3. **Footer differed on every page and from the plan.** One shared `footer.html` with the planned Products, Get started, Support and Company columns, a legal bar and language links. Footer headings are now `h2` instead of skipped-level `h6`.

### Accessibility (WCAG)
4. Page language set to English (1.3.1/3.1.1).
5. Contrast raised: inactive timeline labels, small print, "And in English.", schedule shift end times, Rondo approval rows, kitchen screen footer (1.4.3).
6. Inactive day moments are now `inert`, so keyboard focus can't reach hidden links (2.4.3, 2.4.7).
7. The Rondo answer on the homepage shows at once when it receives focus.
8. The lit statement keeps a screen-reader copy instead of an `aria-label` on a paragraph (4.1.2).
9. Target sizes: logo, section names, language links and small buttons reach at least 24px; header buttons have an invisible 44px hit area (2.5.8).
10. NEVORA's section bar shows "Free trial" instead of "Request a free trial" under 420px.

### UI polish (better-ui)
11. Press feedback unified at `scale(0.96)` and never on disabled buttons.
12. Hover effects run only on devices with a mouse (`@media (hover: hover)`), so taps don't leave stuck states.
13. Tap highlight removed on controls that draw their own pressed state.
14. Motion: transitions name their properties; the one keyframe on a toggled class (wrong-PIN shake) is a one-shot and is off under reduced motion. The 1.1s rise on page headers is a deliberate, consistent convention across pages.

## Shopify readiness

`liquid-proof/velani-start.liquid` converts "How starting works" into an Online Store 2.0 section: editable heading, subheading and button, up to six `step` blocks with `shopify_attributes`, a preset with the four real steps, `{% stylesheet %}` and `{% javascript %}`, and a custom element whose `connectedCallback`/`disconnectedCallback` re-run cleanly when the theme editor re-renders the section. It also drops the mockup's resize script: the progress line is pure CSS from the number of blocks. Shopify's theme check: 0 offenses.

Work the real build still has to do (mockup shortcuts, not defects):
- Replace the `zoom`-based device scaling with CSS sizing.
- Move inline `style=""` attributes (NEVORA 31, Relay 8, home 4) into classes.
- Serve images from `assets/` with `image_url`/`image_tag` instead of data URIs; host Inter locally (no Google Fonts call).
- Put every string in `locales/en.default.json` and `es.json`.
- Turn scroll scenes into native CSS scroll-driven animations with the IntersectionObserver fallback, as in the research report.

Not verified here: real screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA), Safari and Firefox, 200% zoom on every section, and hosted Shopify performance. These belong to the build's browser checks.

# Second review: Plans and About mockups

Same skills as above: design critique, 21st inspiration, better-ui, the WCAG audit and the Shopify Liquid skill. Checked in Chromium with axe-core, the keyboard, 320px and 360px widths, reduced motion and with JavaScript off.

## Result

Good to go as mockups after the fixes below. axe: 0 violations on Plans, About and the homepage once the scroll reveals finish (an axe scan in the middle of a fade-in reports the half-faded colors; the reduced-motion scan, which reads the settled colors, is clean). No horizontal overflow at 320px. All keyboard paths work.

## Findings and fixes

1. **Team-size slider had no visible focus ring** (WCAG 2.4.7). It now shows a 2px ring 4px outside the track.
2. **With JavaScript off, the 30-day calendar showed made-up dates.** It now shows "Day 1, Day 4, Day 11, Day 30" until the script fills in the visitor's real dates.
3. **Unlit headlines were too faint.** The About beliefs and the homepage statement started at 16% opacity before the scroll lit them; the floor is now 50%, so the words stay readable if the visitor stops mid-scroll.
4. **Added: price per employee** under the monthly price (8 employees → $2.38 per employee; 22 → $1.32). Inspired by seat-based pricing sliders found with 21st. It uses only the real prices and hides for 31+.
5. Checked and kept: slider by keyboard (arrows, Home, End; 16 reads "$29 a month"; End reads "More than 30 employees, assisted setup"; − disables at 1, + at 31+), copy-email button, plan card "Your plan" tag following the slider, both endings of the 30 days.

## Shopify readiness

`liquid-proof/velani-plans-calc.liquid` turns the team-size price into an Online Store 2.0 section. The tier limits and prices are number settings, so a price change never needs code. Liquid renders the starting size, price and price per employee on the server, so the section is correct before or without JavaScript. A custom element with `connectedCallback`/`disconnectedCallback` handles the theme editor re-render. Shopify's theme check: 0 offenses (together with `velani-start.liquid`). The Shopify skill's own validator could not run here: its package `@shopify/theme-check-common` is missing from the skill folder.

Not verified here: the section rendered on a real Shopify store, real screen readers, Safari and Firefox.

# Help and Contact mockups: checks before handoff

Built on the same shell as the other pages. Checked in Chromium.

## Result

- **Behavior:** 43 scripted checks pass. Help: search (accent- and case-insensitive), topic chips (mouse and keyboard), both together, the empty state and "Show all answers", one match opens by itself, the PIN fills as its card scrolls in. Contact: each `?interest=` value (and an unknown one) picks the right topic; an empty submit names 2 fields and focuses the first; bad email and locations caught; errors clear as you type and when the topic changes; "More than 30" on a trial offers the switch instead of switching; valid drafts show the confirmation, which says nothing was sent, with focus on its heading; "Edit the draft" keeps what was typed.
- **Accessibility:** axe 0 violations on both pages with settled colors (reduced motion). No horizontal scroll at 320px. Inline links are underlined. The topic radios sit invisibly over their tiles; the tile draws the focus ring, so the audit's "hidden focusable" note for them is expected.
- **Without JavaScript:** Contact falls back to "Tell us what you need." with its button; the form can't be checked without the script, as on the live preview form.

## Fixed during the build

1. Class names that collided with existing CSS (`.srch`, `.chips`, `.incl`, `.ans`, `.ic`, `.nx`, `.tl` and others) were renamed; the collisions had squeezed the search field and restyled the chips.
2. A hint box with `display:flex` showed while `hidden`; both pages now force `[hidden]` to hide.
3. A script ran before its rules were defined (Contact stopped at load); fixed and covered by the checks.
4. Phones: the form now comes right after the heading, before the next steps; the Help chips scroll in one row; the topic tiles stay two-by-two with the icon above the label.
5. Copy held to the facts: the Relay steps no longer promise a confirmation email or "first" access; setup says only that Jose sets it up with you.

## Notes for the theme build

- Contact must keep the live-form safeguards from `src/render.mjs`: Shopify's `{% form 'contact' %}` only when inquiries are enabled on the main theme; the preview check everywhere else.
- The artifact viewer may not pass `?interest=` through to the page; on the real site it does. The mockup also reads `#interest=`.
- axe best-practice notes on other pages (heading order and landmarks inside the fake app screens, the NEVORA section bar outside a landmark) predate this work; the real theme should mark the device screens as images.

Not verified here: real screen readers, Safari and Firefox, the hosted artifact viewer.
