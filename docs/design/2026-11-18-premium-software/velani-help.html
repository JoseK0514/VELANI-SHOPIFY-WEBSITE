If you work at a business that uses NEVORA

How do I sign in?

NEVORA has no shared sign-in page. Each workplace has its own address, and you sign in there with your Employee ID and password, or with a passkey or Face ID where offered. Your address and Employee ID are in the invitation email from your workplace.

I never got an invitation.

Your account comes from your workplace, not from this website. Ask your Owner to send your invitation.

I forgot my password.

Choose Forgot password? on your workplace’s sign-in page. If you no longer have the email address on your account, ask your manager or Owner: they can confirm it’s you and give you a recovery code. We never ask for your password or a verification code by email.

I forgot my PIN.

Your 4-digit PIN is only for clocking in on your workplace’s tablet. It isn’t your password. Ask the Owner to set a new one.

A shift, a time-off request or my pay looks wrong.

Ask your manager or the Owner. Your workplace manages shifts, time off, pay and corrections to work records. Write to us if a problem in the app stops you from reaching them.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete my account. Read what is removed and what may be kept, then re-enter your password to confirm. No app? Sign in on the web at your workplace’s address and do the same. See the four steps.

If you own the business

How does my business start?

Request a free trial. Online signup isn’t open yet: Jose from Velani replies within two business days and sets up your workplace with you. Your 30 free days start the day your workplace is ready, and no card is needed.

What does it cost after 30 days?

$19 a month for up to 15 employees, or $29 a month for 16 to 30. One price for the whole business, billed monthly in USD. Owners aren’t counted. More than 30 employees? Write to us for assisted setup.

What happens on day 30?

The Owner picks a plan in NEVORA. Nothing is charged until you do. Without a plan, NEVORA pauses for your team: you can still open billing and export available payroll records, and employees can still see their own pay statements. Ending a trial doesn’t delete anything on its own.

Does NEVORA pay my employees?

No. NEVORA calculates pay statements from the hours you approve. You still pay your team and file payroll taxes.

Can Rondo change the schedule on its own?

No. Rondo prepares a change and shows what will be different. Nothing changes until you approve it, and NEVORA checks the change again before it applies. Rondo is for the Owner only.

When is Relay available?

Relay is in design, with no date and no price yet. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when early access opens.

How do I close my workplace?

Email support@thevelani.com and we’ll help you with your workplace and its records.
