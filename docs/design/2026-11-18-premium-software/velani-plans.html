Questions

Who counts as an employee?

Every person on your team in NEVORA. Owners aren't counted.

How am I billed?

Monthly, per business, in US dollars. One price covers the whole app for everyone on your team.

Can the prices change?

Prices may change before online billing opens.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. NEVORA keeps working through the month you paid for.

What if I have more than 30 employees?

Write to us for assisted setup. We reply within two business days.

Does NEVORA pay my employees or file payroll taxes?

No. NEVORA calculates pay statements from the hours you approve. You still pay your team and file payroll taxes.
