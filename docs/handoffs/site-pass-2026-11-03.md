## Contact page, third pass (Jose: "this looks AI and is slop")

The two-column form with numbered groups, a "What happens next" column and a lock line was rejected. The 21st.dev catalog search for contact forms returned eight variations of that same card-and-fields pattern, which is the point: it is the default everyone ships.

The replacement is one sentence the visitor completes, set in display type on the page itself:

> My name is ___ from ___. I need [a website ▾]. Reply to me at ___.
> What needs to work better: ___

For a NEVORA quote or the free demo the sentence changes: "We have [some ▾] people on shifts at ___ locations. Set up our free NEVORA demo and reply to me at ___."

- No card, no columns, no labels above fields, no icons. Every blank is a real input with a visually hidden label; blanks grow to what is typed (`field-sizing: content` with a measured fallback in `velani.js`). Selects read as words with a small caret. The message is one ruled line that grows.
- Errors list under the sentence in plain words and the first blank gets focus; the blank's underline turns red.
- Under the button: one line, "We reply by email within two business days. Privacy". The demo keeps its day-30 and payroll precision as small print. Support facts sit on one hairline-topped line at the end.
- New topic value `website` accepted by `src/inquiry.mjs` (the sentence offers "a website" and "a custom app" separately).
- Footer links now match the header (NEVORA · Websites · Custom apps · Plans · Support · About · Contact) and sit in two columns on phones.
- `scripts/checks/clipping.mjs` now skips `.sr` elements, which are 1px by design.
- The ui-ux-pro-max search database is not installed in this checkout (only its SKILL.md), so its guidance came from the priority table, not a database match.
