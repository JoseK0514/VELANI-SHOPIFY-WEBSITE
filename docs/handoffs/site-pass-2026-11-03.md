## The system (src/night/base.css)

- One button: square (4px), uppercase, letter-spaced, 50px, with an arrow. Outlined by default (`.hb`, `.pill.line`), filled white for the primary action (`.hb.fill`, `.pill`). `.pill` gets its arrow from `assets/arrow-right.svg` through a mask; the header, cookie dialog and assistant opt out.
- Uppercase labels (`.kicker`, `.hkick`) above every title, in the header links, the NEVORA bar, the footer nav, form labels, facts (`.spec dt`) and step numbers.
- The full-screen panel system (`.hpanel`, `.hph`, `.hshade`, `.hcopy`, `.hshot`, `.hweb`) moved from home.css to base.css so every page can use it. `.hpanel.long` for a long h1, `.hpanel.dim` for a darker wash, `.hpanel.end` for the closing panel.
- Surfaces: 12px panels, 4px controls, 1px light edges. No accent-blue fills; the accent stays for links and the current menu item.
- Phone menu: full-screen sheet, uppercase rows, "Start free NEVORA demo" and the client-support line at the bottom.

## Polish pass (same day, Jose's follow-up)

- **Home hero**: the opening image drifts slowly (`.hpanel.live .hph`, a 30 s scale and pan, alternating), a chevron at the bottom invites the first scroll (`.hcue`, links to `#nevora`), and the opening panel now ends at the bottom of the first screen (`calc(100svh - 56px)`). Reduced motion gets a still image and a still chevron.
- **Legal and policy pages** (Jose named these as the old UI): numbered sections with no hairlines, a quiet contents column with a 1px edge, white underlined links, a "Questions" block with a button instead of the grey contact line; the legal index is a grid of flat tiles. Policy text untouched.
- **Links**: every href on every route (55 distinct) was fetched and its fragment checked; nothing broken. The browser suite does the same on each run.
- **Smoothness**: button glass (`backdrop-filter`) kept only on hero buttons; header and NEVORA bar blur lightened (14px, no saturate); the home and NEVORA hero images are preloaded; below-fold images decode asynchronously; anchors scroll smoothly (off under reduced motion). The scroll-stability suite reports CLS 0 and no long frames on 8 of 9 routes (NEVORA: 3 frames over 50 ms on an emulated phone, below the 15-frame threshold).
