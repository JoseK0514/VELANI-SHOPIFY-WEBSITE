# Velani entrance: material study

The original thin mint V, spaced wordmark and curtain reveal were rejected by the owner as generic and insufficiently premium. This revision changes the visual direction substantially, while preserving immediate dismissal and an accessible route to the page.

Audience and purpose: first-time visitors meeting Velani as a technology company and discovering NEVORA. One brief, authored brand moment before the actual content.

Palette: porcelain #ecede9, warm white #fcfcf7, graphite #171a19, polished silver #bdc5c1, restrained reflected teal #5c8078. Runtime stage colors live in public/entrance.css; physical lighting parameters live in public/entrance-renderer.js.

Type: Manrope medium, tightly optically spaced and exceptionally large for VELANI; the same face at a quiet 10–11px for the product identifier and the Skip action. The wider application retains its existing reading typography.

Layout: a single machined sculpture fills the upper stage, overlapping a large wordmark near the bottom. A quiet NEVORA identifier is the only secondary copy. Skip is continuously available in the upper-right corner. Mobile keeps a substantial sculpture and full-width wordmark.

Signature: two broad bevelled titanium ribbons form the V. A close camera pulls back and turns the material into a recognizable mark. The wordmark rises through one baseline mask. Finally, the camera moves toward the sculpture and a triangular opening in the same direction reveals the real page.

Timing: 0–1.4s macro pullback and rotation; 0.65–1.8s typographic reveal; 1.8–2.25s composed hold; 2.25–3.3s forward camera movement and triangular reveal. Cleanup at 3.35s. No sound or invented loading percentage.

Critique: chrome alone is a common trend. The refinement is the specific machined two-part V, broad studio reflections, low-key seam, and an exit derived from its negative space. Avoid rainbow chrome, floating particles, neon, card stacks and individually bouncing letters. Do not describe aesthetic quality as established until the owner has seen the revision.

Technical baseline: standalone WebGL with a vector material fallback; no libraries, videos, remote image requests or backend dependencies. Rendering only runs while the dialog is open. Skip, Escape, Tab, first-visit marker, focus/scroll restoration and reduced-motion bypass remain intact. This is a marketing surface, not a redesign of application forms or workflows.
