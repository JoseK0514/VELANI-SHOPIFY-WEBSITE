# A gradual shift-swap and timesheet plan for three cafés

NEVORA · VELANI

Your three cafés have 42 people, with shift swaps arranged by group text and paper timesheets approved by two managers every Friday\. Suggested approach: keep those familiar tools initially, introduce one clear swap-confirmation process, and standardize Friday reviews and the payroll handoff\. Try the process at one café before extending it to the other two, then assess whether software would fit\.

## Business details used for this plan
- Team size: 42
- Main challenge: Want a clearer shift-swap and timesheet process across three cafés\.
- Locations: Three cafés
- Constraints: Prefer gradual changes rather than changing everything at once\.
- Business type: Cafés
- Current tools: Group text for shift swaps; paper timesheets
- Approval timing: Two managers approve paper timesheets every Friday\.

## One connected workflow.

### Plan shifts

Described product workflow: NEVORA's described workflow lets managers plan the week on the web and employees view shifts, respond to shift-swap offers and request time off on their phones\. NEVORA is in development; availability for a particular business requires confirmation\.

A suggestion for your team: Suggestion: keep group texts for swap requests, but designate a manager to confirm each change and update one agreed schedule\. Use a consistent request format covering the café, shift and proposed replacement\. Start at one café rather than changing all three at once\.

### Capture time

Described product workflow: The described NEVORA time clock runs on a shared tablet, uses a separate four-digit PIN for each worker and clears between people\. Its interface supports English and Spanish; this does not establish a bilingual platform\. The inspected draft demonstration did not save attendance records, and its public availability is unverified\.

A suggestion for your team: Suggestion: retain paper timesheets initially and agree on a consistent format for actual start and finish times, breaks and reported corrections\. Keep actual hours separate from planned shifts, so an unconfirmed swap does not prevent someone from recording time worked\. Review discrepancies before changing a record\.

### Approve hours

Described product workflow: NEVORA's published scope includes approved hours, and managers are described as approving hours on the web\. The reviewed sources do not establish a two-manager approval sequence or configurable Friday approval rules\.

A suggestion for your team: Suggestion: preserve Friday approvals\. Agree which manager reviews each café and who handles unresolved questions, avoiding duplicate or missed reviews\. Use one checklist for missing entries, actual hours and corrections\. Record reviewer sign-off and keep unresolved items visible for prompt follow-up without withholding earned pay\.

### Prepare payroll

Described product workflow: NEVORA is described as calculating payroll from approved hours, with a manager reviewing calculations and publishing employee pay statements\. It does not pay employees or file payroll taxes\. Supported payroll integrations, export formats, overtime rules and deductions are not verified in the reviewed sources\.

A suggestion for your team: Suggestion: keep your existing payroll arrangements while standardizing the handoff of reviewed timesheets\. Include approval status and any outstanding questions, retain the original records, and have the person responsible for payroll verify the handoff\. Check compatibility before considering any software change\.

## Make the next steps your own

1. Agree on swap confirmation and review ownership
   Who owns this?: Operations manager
   When?: Suggested: week one
   Choose one café for an initial trial\. Agree who confirms swaps, where the current schedule is kept, and which manager reviews each café's timesheets\. Keep group texting, but distinguish a proposed swap from a confirmed schedule change\.

2. Trial a consistent paper timesheet and Friday checklist
   Who owns this?: Suggested: manager responsible for the trial café
   When?: Suggested: first Friday review after setup
   Use the same time-entry fields and review checklist for the trial café\. Check reported hours against the schedule only to identify questions, not to replace actual hours with scheduled hours\. Review corrections with the worker concerned and preserve the original entry\.

3. Clarify the handoff from approval to payroll
   Who owns this?: Suggested: approving managers and person responsible for payroll
   When?: Suggested: before the next payroll handoff
   Agree what reviewed records payroll needs, how approval is shown and who follows up on discrepancies\. Keep payment and tax responsibilities within the existing payroll process\. Confirm that Friday reviews fit the payroll deadline\.

4. Review the trial before expanding
   Who owns this?: Suggested: the two managers
   When?: Suggested: after two Friday review cycles
   Discuss whether staff can identify confirmed swaps and whether timesheet questions reach the right reviewer\. Adjust the process, then consider extending it to the other cafés\. Treat any later NEVORA evaluation as a separate decision subject to availability and workflow fit\.

## Assumptions to review
- The suggested first phase assumes you can retain group texts and paper timesheets while introducing shared process rules\.
- A trial at one café is a suggestion, not a confirmed rollout decision\.

## Questions to resolve
- Do both managers review every timesheet, or do they divide responsibility by café or team?
- How do approved Friday timesheets reach payroll, and what deadline must that handoff meet?
