Questions

Who counts as an employee?{{icon:caret-down}}

Every person on your team in NEVORA. Owners aren’t counted.

How am I billed?{{icon:caret-down}}

Monthly, per business, in US dollars. One price covers the whole app for everyone on your team.

Can the prices change?{{icon:caret-down}}

Prices are in US dollars and may change before online billing opens.

Can I cancel?{{icon:caret-down}}

Yes, any time. NEVORA keeps working through the month you paid for.

What if I have more than 30 employees?{{icon:caret-down}}

Write to us for assisted setup. We review what you need and give you a quote; teams we approve get the same 30 days free.

Does NEVORA pay my employees or file payroll taxes?{{icon:caret-down}}

No. NEVORA calculates pay statements from the hours you approve. It does not pay employees or file payroll taxes.
