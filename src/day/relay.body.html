What we're designing

Three jobs. One system.

Relay is being designed for the rush: the order, the kitchen, and what's running low.

Orders

Take the order once.

At the counter or for pickup. The order goes straight to the kitchen with its notes.

  • ham and cheese

The kitchen screen

Every ticket, in order, with a timer.

The kitchen sees what to make next and how long each order has waited. Bump it when it's ready.
