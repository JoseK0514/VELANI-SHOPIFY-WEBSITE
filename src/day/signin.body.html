How to sign in to NEVORA.

NEVORA has no shared sign-in page. Each workplace has its own address, and you sign in there with your Employee ID and password.

  • {{icon:envelope-simple}}

    Your address and ID

    They’re in the invitation email from your workplace. Lost it? Ask your employer.

  • {{icon:key}}

    Forgot your password?

    Choose Forgot password? on your workplace’s sign-in page. You’ll need your Employee ID and the email your workplace has on file.

  • {{icon:device-tablet}}

    Your 4-digit PIN

    It’s only for clocking in on your workplace’s tablet, not for signing in. Forgot it? Ask the business Owner to set a new one.
