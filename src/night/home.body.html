NEVORA is our app for scheduling, the time clock and payroll calculation, made for teams that work in shifts. We also build web and mobile apps, business systems and integrations for other companies.

In development · Offered to businesses by quote

NEVORA covers the week, from the posted schedule to each person’s pay statement.

Managers plan the week and approve hours on the web. Employees check their shifts, answer swap offers and ask for time off from their phone. At work, each person clocks in on a shared tablet with their own PIN, in English or Spanish. When the pay period closes, NEVORA calculates payroll for you to review, then publishes pay statements.
