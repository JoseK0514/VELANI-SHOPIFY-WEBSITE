Questions

Can my business use NEVORA now?{{icon:plus}}

NEVORA is in development and offered to businesses by quote. Tell us about your team and we’ll discuss its availability.

Does NEVORA pay my employees or file payroll taxes?{{icon:plus}}

No. NEVORA calculates payroll from approved hours and publishes pay statements. Your business continues to pay employees and file payroll taxes.

How do employees get access?{{icon:plus}}

Your business gives them access. They sign in on their phone or the web, and use their own four-digit PIN at the workplace tablet.

Which languages does the time clock support?{{icon:plus}}

The time clock works in English and Spanish.

I use NEVORA at work and need help.{{icon:plus}}

Ask your manager first. For signing in or your account, see NEVORA support.
