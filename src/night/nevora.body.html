NEVORA: Scheduling, time clock and payroll for teams that work in shifts.

Managers plan the week and approve hours on the web. Employees see their shifts and send requests from their phone. Everyone clocks in on a shared tablet with their own PIN. When the pay period closes, NEVORA calculates payroll for your review and publishes pay statements.

What happens when the pay period closes

NEVORA calculates payroll from the hours you approved. You review it, and when you publish, each employee finds their pay statement under My pay, on their phone or the web.

NEVORA does

  • Calculates payroll from approved hours
  • Shows it to you for review first
  • Publishes each person’s pay statement

You keep doing

  • Paying your team, the way you do today
  • Filing payroll taxes

Questions businesses ask

Can my business start using NEVORA now?{{icon:plus}}

NEVORA is in development. Ask for a quote and we’ll tell you where things stand for a team like yours.

Does NEVORA pay my employees or file payroll taxes?{{icon:plus}}

No. NEVORA calculates payroll from the hours you approve and publishes pay statements. You pay your team and file payroll taxes the way you do today.

How much does NEVORA cost?{{icon:plus}}

We price NEVORA for each business, based on what you tell us about your team. Asking for a quote doesn’t commit you to anything.

Is the time clock in Spanish?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. The time clock works in English and Spanish, and the Español button is on the PIN screen.

How does the time clock keep each person’s hours separate?{{icon:plus}}

Each person has their own PIN. After each clock-in, the tablet shows the time it saved, then clears the screen for the next person.

Where can I read how NEVORA handles employee information?{{icon:plus}}

In the NEVORA privacy policy. It explains what is collected, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to ask for deletion.

I use NEVORA at work. How do I get an account?{{icon:plus}}

Your account comes from your employer, so ask your manager first. If you still need help, write to support@thevelani.com.
