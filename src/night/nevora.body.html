Built for work

Works on
The web, phones and a shared tablet at the workplace for the time clock.
Languages
The time clock works in English and Spanish.
Signing in
Each person has their own account. At the tablet they use a separate four-digit PIN, and the screen clears itself for the next person.
Payroll
Calculated from the hours you approve. You review it before each pay statement is published. NEVORA doesn’t pay your team or file payroll taxes.
Where data lives
Database and application servers in the United States, in Northern Virginia.
Deleting an account
In the app or on the web. We aim to complete eligible requests within 30 calendar days.

Access is role-based, connections are encrypted, and security and access logs are kept for up to 90 days. NEVORA does not sell personal information or track people across other companies’ apps and sites for advertising. Details are in the NEVORA privacy policy.

Questions businesses ask

Can my business start using NEVORA now?{{icon:plus}}

NEVORA is in development. Ask for a quote and we will explain its current availability for a team like yours.

Does NEVORA pay my employees or file payroll taxes?{{icon:plus}}

No. NEVORA calculates payroll from the hours you approve and publishes pay statements. You pay your team and file payroll taxes the way you do today.

How much does NEVORA cost?{{icon:plus}}

We price NEVORA for each business, based on what you tell us about your team. A quote does not commit you to anything.

Where can I read how NEVORA handles employee information?{{icon:plus}}

In the NEVORA privacy policy. It explains what is collected, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to ask for deletion.

I use NEVORA at work. How do I get an account?{{icon:plus}}

Your account comes from your employer, so ask your manager first. If you still need help, write to support@thevelani.com.
