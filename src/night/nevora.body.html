Questions

Questions businesses ask

Can my business start using NEVORA now?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Start the free demo and we set up your workplace. We are still finishing a few features, and we tell you which.

Does NEVORA pay my employees or file payroll taxes?{{icon:plus}}

No. NEVORA calculates payroll from approved hours and publishes pay statements. You pay your team and file taxes as you do today.

How much does NEVORA cost?{{icon:plus}}

One price per business after the free 30 days, set by quote. See Plans.

Ask your manager; accounts come from your workplace. Still stuck? support@thevelani.com.
