Questions

Can my business start using NEVORA now?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Ask for a free trial and tell us about your team. We reply within two business days and set up your workplace with you; online signup isn’t open yet. Your 30 free days start the day your workplace is ready.

Does NEVORA pay my employees or file payroll taxes?{{icon:plus}}

No. NEVORA calculates payroll from approved hours and publishes pay statements. You pay your team and file taxes as you do today.

How much does NEVORA cost?{{icon:plus}}

After the free 30 days, $19 a month for up to 15 employees or $29 a month for 16 to 30, for the whole app. More than 30 employees? Write to us for assisted setup. See Plans.

I use NEVORA at work. How do I get an account?{{icon:plus}}

Ask your manager; accounts come from your workplace. Still stuck? support@thevelani.com.
